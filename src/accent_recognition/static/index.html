Accent Recognition

Record or upload a few seconds of English speech. A small neural network, written from scratch in NumPy, guesses the accent.

Record 3 to 15 seconds. Speak in a normal voice.

The model is not sure about this clip. Treat the result as a weak guess.
What to expect. The model knows a few accents. It trained on read speech from the VCTK corpus (74 speakers). On speakers it has never heard, it scores about 54% balanced accuracy, where random guessing scores 33%. It is a portfolio project, not a reliable tool. Audio is processed in memory and is not stored.
